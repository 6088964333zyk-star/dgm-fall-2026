abstract: >
  The second half of the course turns from discriminative models, which learn P(Y | X), to generative models, which learn the joint P(X, Y). This lecture compares the two paths through logistic regression and Naive Bayes, revisits MAP and regularization, and discusses when each approach wins. It then introduces autoencoders as a way to learn low-dimensional representations, along with denoising, sparse, and variational variants.
